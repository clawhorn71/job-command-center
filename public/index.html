Product guide

          
One board for every role your Grok Bots touch

          

            Job Command Center is the desk view for Carter’s search. Applications,
            recruiter status, and LinkedIn suggestions land here so you can act
            without hunting through three bot threads.
          

        

        

          

            01
            
Home

            

              The pipeline is grouped by status: Applied, Screening, Interview, and Closed.
              Use the search field to filter. Change a role’s status from the dropdown on the card.
            

          

          

            02
            
LinkedIn suggestions

            

              Apply opens the real posting (LinkedIn or the company ATS) and
              moves the role onto Applied. Remove hides listings that are not a
              fit so they stay off the board on the next sync.
            

          

          

            03
            
Metrics

            

              Overview, Funnel, and Companies show rates, mix, weekly volume, and where
              suggestions concentrate. Use this when you want the shape of the search, not every card.
            

          

          

            04
            
Connect bots

            

              Grok Bot has no public read API. Paste the prompts into Job Tracker, LinkedIn Job Bot,
              and Job Email Bot, and enable Execution on Local Computer. Or drop JSON into
              data/inbox/ / import from chat.
            

          

        

        

          
Keep it current

          

            Leave this app running on this Mac. After each scrape or inbox pass, the bots POST
            to the local ingest URL. Home refreshes on its own. Collapsing the side menu (the
            chevron next to Command Center) keeps the board full-width when you want more columns.
